What this is

We opened Blank Spaces the way a new user does, then rebuilt the parts we think are leaving money on the table.

Adapty powers subscriptions for 30,000+ apps, so we get to see what actually moves conversion and ARPU across the market rather than inside one app. We pointed that at Blank Spaces: installed it, went through the onboarding and the set-up, read your App Store listing and your website, and looked for gaps between what you already have and what you currently show people.

What follows is five ideas — not fifteen. Each one is a hypothesis you can A/B test, and each one is built into a clickable prototype so you can see it rather than imagine it. The prototype uses your design, not ours.

We did not redesign your app

Every screen in the prototype is yours — the falling lines, the outlined answer rows, the life-in-weeks grid and its 27 years, the launcher mockup, the plan cards with HOTTEST and Claim Offer, the theme picker. Only the order changed, plus what the screens say back. This is not a design pitch.

You may have tested some of this

We are working from the outside, so we cannot see your experiment history. If one of these is something you have already run, tell us — we will go deeper on the rest rather than repeat it.
