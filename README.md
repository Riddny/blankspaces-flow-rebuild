# Growth Audit — Blank Spaces

**Live:** https://riddny.github.io/blankspaces-flow-rebuild/

Five ranked ideas to unlock more revenue from the traffic Blank Spaces already has, plus a clickable prototype of the onboarding and paywall rebuilt in the app's own design. Prepared by Adapty.

| # | Idea | Potential outcome |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| 01 | Offer Lifetime on its own when they close — $23.99, one payment | ARPU +10–15% |
| 02 | Name their goal in the paywall headline | CR +15–20% |
| 03 | Name what steals their time under the 27 years | CR +10–20% |
| 04 | Put your 4.6 stars on the paywall | CR +10–15% |
| 05 | Read their answers back before the 27 years | CR +10–15% |

Every price, rating, review and feature line comes from Blank Spaces' own App Store listing, website and in-app screens. Impact ranges are Adapty's expected effect from teardowns and A/B tests across subscription apps — not measured lift for Blank Spaces.
